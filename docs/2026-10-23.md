- **Ranking metrics:** HN score 504
- **Quick summary:** HackiOS boots Apple's open-source XNU kernel (xnu-8796.121.2, the kernel of iOS 16.5) on Android phone hardware and runs the unmodified iOS 16.5 user space on top of it: launchd → backboardd → SpringBoard → apps. It runs in two places: - POCO X5 Pro 5G (redwood, Snapdragon 778G) — nothing is flashed: the UEFI is started with fastboot boot, the whole system lives in RAM, and after a reboot the phone is back in Android; - QEMU (virt, aarch64) — the same thing on any Linux computer, no phone needed.
