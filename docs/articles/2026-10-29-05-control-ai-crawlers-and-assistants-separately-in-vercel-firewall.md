# Control AI crawlers and assistants separately in Vercel Firewall

## Summary

You can now log or block requests from AI crawlers and AI assistants separately with the AI Bots managed ruleset in Vercel Firewall. - AI crawlers: bots that collect content for indexing and model training - AI assistants: bots that fetch pages in response to user actions or search queries For example, you can block AI crawlers while allowing AI assistants to retrieve your content to answer users’ questions. Vercel updates the rules as new bots are added.

## Key Takeaways

- To configure these controls, open your project’s Security > Firewall > Rules.
- Under Bot Management, enable AI Bots and select the gear icon beside it.
- Set an action for AI crawlers and AI assistants, then review and publish your changes.
