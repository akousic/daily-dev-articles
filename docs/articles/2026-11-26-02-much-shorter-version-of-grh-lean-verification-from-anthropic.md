## Summary

Not maintained and not accepting contributions. A Lean 4 proof of the Riemann Hypothesis and of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions, built on Mathlib and using only the axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. The proof is based on Levent Alpöge's, with Claude (https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf).
