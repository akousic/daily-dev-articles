LLM Voice 29 October 2026 Sometimes when I read a post, I get the growing feeling that I'm reading the output of an LLM. I can't necessarily define why, although there are the common tells (it's not the em-dashes, it's the load-bearing tapestries). As I do this my desire to read on atrophies, sometimes to the point of revulsion There are tools out there that claim to be able to tell the difference between LLM-written prose and the old-fashioned biological product.

Key Takeaways

  • The reaction to LLM-Voice is a human one, that sense that we are in an uncanny valley of prose.
