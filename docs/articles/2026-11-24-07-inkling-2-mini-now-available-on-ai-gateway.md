## Summary

Inkling 2 Mini from Thinking Machines is now available on AI Gateway. The small multimodal reasoning model is built for agentic coding, tool use, and knowledge work, with text and image inputs and text output. Inkling 2 Mini has 26.4 billion total parameters and 3.4 billion active per token.

## Key Takeaways

- The model runs its middle layers more than once for every token, which lets it reason further before writing anything down and often reach an answer with fewer reasoning tokens.
- Like Inkling 2 Small, it is strongest on long-running agentic tasks.
- The model has a 1M-token context window, and reasoning effort can be set from none through max.
