## Summary

Autonomous teams are an article of faith in modern software development. The shape of our value determines what we can do; it determines our trade-offs between agency and coherence. We all have a purpose, and we all have agency to do something, Simon Rohrer explained in his talk essence and accident in product development complexity at Craft conference.
