# YouTube Ad Punisher – Auto-reloads YouTube pages when an ad plays

## Summary

Auto-reloads YouTube pages when an ad plays — preserving your playback position. Ad density on YouTube is channel-dependent and changes over time — some channels front-load several ads, others run none. Traditional ad blockers can get detected by YouTube's anti-adblock system ("Ad blockers are not allowed here"), and the skip button takes a few seconds to appear.

## Key Takeaways

- You sit there, hostage.
- Two reloads and the ad is gone.
- 🎯 YouTube Ad Punisher automates this: the moment an ad appears, it captures your exact playback timestamp, reloads the page, and YouTube auto-seeks back to where you were.
