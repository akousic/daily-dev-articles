## Summary

Last week, MIT CSAIL published Sparks of Weight-Space Programming. Given the raw weights of a small network, GPT-6 Astra could tell which game it had been trained to play. Given only a game’s rules, it could write the weights of a small network from scratch and beat networks trained with PPO for 100 million steps.

## Key Takeaways

- No gradients, no training loop, just reasoning about what the weights compute.
- They coined this weight-space programming.
- In another experiment, they asked Astra to make Qwen3.5-0.8B better at math by editing its weights.
