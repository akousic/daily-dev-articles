Fish Audio models now available on Vercel AI Gateway for free

VercelRank #6 · RSS curated source · 2026-08-20 14:43 UTC

Open original article ↗

Summary

Fish Audio's audio models are now available on AI Gateway. To celebrate the launch, every Fish Audio model is free on AI Gateway for the next 30 days, through September 18. Four models from Fish Audio are available, including their latest text-to-speech model: - fish-audio/s2.1-pro (text-to-speech): Built for low-latency streaming; clones a voice from a reference recording.

Key Takeaways

  • - fish-audio/transcribe-1 (transcription): Returns the text along with the duration of the audio and timestamped segments, down to individual words.
  • - fish-audio/s2-pro (text-to-speech): Covers around eighty languages and takes inline tags, plain-language directions written into the text itself, so you can change how a single word or phrase is delivered instead of setting one style for the whole request.
  • - fish-audio/s1 (text-to-speech): Reads text that can carry markers for emotion, tone, and sound effects.
