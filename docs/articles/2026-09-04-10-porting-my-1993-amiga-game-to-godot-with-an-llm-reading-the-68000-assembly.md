## Summary

In 1993, in Baghdad, I built a game called Babylonian Twins on an Amiga 500: 512KB of RAM, no hard drive, plugged into a TV. I was an engineering student in my twenties. Pure 68000 assembly, every sprite and every scanline by hand.

## Key Takeaways

- Murtadha Salman drew the art and Mahir AlSalman composed the music.
- We were under sanctions.
- No internet, no game development resources, just one copy of the Amiga Hardware Reference Manual, which I used to program the hardware directly, and electricity a few hours a day.
