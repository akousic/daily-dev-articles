## Summary

Beam from Reflection AI, previously available in stealth as stealth/cat-jam, is now available on AI Gateway. Beam is free to use for a limited time during the launch period when serviced by Reflection AI. Beam is an low latency US open-weight reasoning model for coding and agentic tasks, with weights released under Apache 2.0.

## Key Takeaways

- Beam supports tool calling and structured outputs, with a 1M-token context window for code, documents, and conversation history.
- Reasoning effort is adjustable.
- Beam is designed for speed, activating only 23B of its 501B parameters per token and avoiding tokens a task doesn't need, so it tends to reach answers sooner than larger open models.
