## Summary

At QCon San Francisco 2026, Scott Behrens, Principal Security Engineer at Netflix, presented how staff-plus engineers can define and align a company-wide technical strategy. Drawing on Netflix's experience scaling internal AI agents, the talk argued that a strategy that brings people together can often scale impact further than any code an engineer could write alone. Behrens explained that the work started when several product strategies, each with its own direction, began to share similar technical risks.

## Key Takeaways

- Without a common technical strategy underneath them, teams were optimizing locally for different goals.
- His view was that technical strategy can complement product strategy without waiting for it, that lightweight strategies deliver faster than long technical documents, and that a clear north star does more steering than people expect.
- The talk presented a playbook organized as levels in a video game.
