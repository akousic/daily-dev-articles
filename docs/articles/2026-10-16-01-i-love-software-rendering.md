## Summary

I love software rendering. The best DOS experience I’ve had in a long while was Quake in its software renderer at 800×600, running at a locked 60fps on my Dell OptiPlex 380. It’s one of the best-looking things I’ve seen a DOS game do.

## Key Takeaways

- That’s not how most people remember those games looking.
- In the 90s you either ran them in software at a low resolution, or you bought a 3D card and played the accelerated version.
- Today there’s a third option: run the software renderer on a machine fast enough to push it at resolutions nobody could use back then.
