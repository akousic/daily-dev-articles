Summary

In late October, a slew of major cybersecurity issues came to light. But none were bigger than the so-called PHP2Shell vulnerability, which put a plethora of websites, including those of NASA, Wikipedia, the White House, and all other sites that run on Wordpress or Drupal (among others) at risk of exploits capable of both reading sensitive data and running malicious code on the host server. As far as anyone is aware, disaster has been averted: The hack was discovered by cybersecurity researchers who reported it to the PHP Foundation, and a patch was released before any bad actors got their hands on the exploit.
