AI agents ask better questions than KPMG accountants in audit trial

Hacker NewsRank #1 · HN score 480 · 2026-11-18 15:49 UTC

Summary

LONDON, November 18, 2026 — Artificial intelligence has taken another significant step into the world of professional services after KPMG revealed that AI agents examining the accounts of a major UK-listed company sometimes investigated financial issues more deeply and asked better questions than its human audit team. The experiment, conducted by KPMG UK using more than 100 AI agents, marks an important development in the application of artificial intelligence to one of the most labour-intensive areas of the financial services industry. It also raises an uncomfortable question for the accountancy profession: if machines can increasingly perform the analytical work traditionally undertaken by highly trained professionals, how quickly will the economics of auditing change?

Key Takeaways

  • According to the Financial Times, the project, codenamed Project Exponential, examined the financial statements of an unnamed FTSE 250 company.
  • The AI agents worked alongside a conventional audit team, undertaking tasks across the audit process rather than simply assisting with isolated administrative functions.
