## Summary

Have it both ways: stay discoverable in search while disallowing AI training Without proper controls, website owners have long faced a difficult tradeoff: allow your content to be used for AI training, or risk losing discoverability in search. That tradeoff exists because some of the largest organizations on the Internet use mixed-use crawlers: a single crawler serving both search and AI training. Refuse one, and you refuse the other.

## Key Takeaways

- Today, Cloudflare is announcing a new Disallow AI Training setting that lets you easily stay indexed for search while refusing to let that same crawler train on your content.
- Apple, Google, and Microsoft honor or have committed (in a specified time frame) to honor this setting.
- Mixed-use crawlers were the hard part of the training question.
