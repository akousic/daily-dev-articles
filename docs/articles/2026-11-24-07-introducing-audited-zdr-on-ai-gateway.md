## Summary

AI is now used in every part of a business, and as a result, companies send source code, customer records, and regulated data through inference providers. The assurance that no data is retained after processing is still publicly unverified. Payment platforms, cloud infrastructure, and most other layers that handle data this sensitive have a way to prove their controls to outside parties, and inference will follow the same path.

## Key Takeaways

- Today we are introducing Audited ZDR on AI Gateway.
- A provider is labeled Audited ZDR only after an independent security firm tests its zero data retention claims against its code, infrastructure, and live traffic, then publishes the report.
- Baseten, Modal, and Wafer are launch partners that have already begun audits, and more providers are encouraged to join to help raise the bar for security and privacy.
