## Summary

Technical debt is not necessarily a sign of poor engineering. It can be the price of learning through software in use. As AI accelerates code production beyond our capacity to understand code and context, technical debt increasingly becomes a sociotechnical challenge, Xin Yao noted in her talk "What If Your Biggest Technical Debt Is Social", at GOTO Copenhagen.

## Key Takeaways

- Yao quoted Ward Cunningham, who defined debt as "not-quite-right code".
- Debt sounds like a negative thing, but it can be a deliberate business strategy, she explained: If we release the code now, well knowing it’s not perfect for the future yet, we’ll get some business feedback, we’ll get some user feedback, we’ll probably get paid for something, and that’s good as a business strategy.
- Debt isn’t about poorly written code; it arises when we design software with incomplete knowledge.
