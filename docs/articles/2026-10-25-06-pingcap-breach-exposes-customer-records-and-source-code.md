## Summary

PingCAP recently confirmed unauthorized access to its internal systems and the theft of source code and customer-related data. The attacker, cyber extortion group FulcrumSec, claimed to have stolen 10.6 TB of data, including TiDB Cloud control-plane records, customer SQL statements, API keys, connection logs, billing information, internal documents, and 413 private source-code repositories. These claims have not been independently verified.

## Key Takeaways

- FulcrumSec alleges that access continued undetected for many weeks and that some exposed credentials could provide access to customer systems.
- They write: We possess the complete TiDB Cloud control plane, its billing and finance estate, its GCP storage estate and its AWS cost and billing estate, its Jira, its CRM, its analytics, its source code, and a four-year log of every connection every customer ever made to its product, from the IP addresses of their application servers.
- PingCAP is the company behind TiDB, the open-source distributed SQL database compatible with MySQL.
