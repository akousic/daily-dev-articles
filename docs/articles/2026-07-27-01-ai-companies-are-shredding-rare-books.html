AI companies are shredding rare books

Hacker NewsRank #1 · HN score 587 · 2026-07-27 12:32 UTC

Open original article ↗

Summary

🦔AI companies are bulk-buying rare books, scanning them through high-speed machines that cut the spines off, and shredding the originals. A service called ISBNdb facilitates orders of up to a million books and keeps buyers anonymous. Pre-2022 books are premium because they're free of AI-generated text.

Key Takeaways

  • A federal judge ruled the practice is fair use because eliminating the original means only one copy exists at a time.
  • Anthropic hired the former head of Google Books partnerships to obtain "all the books in the world." My Take This got to me.
  • A bookseller told 404 Media that rare books with almost no surviving copies are being fed into this pipeline.
