Key Takeaways

  • But then before dessert was served, we saw that OpenAI claims to prove that ω, the exponent of matrix multiplication, is exactly 9/4—a lower bound (!!), matching the upper bound that OpenAI proved in the great Mathocalypse drop of October 6.
  • There are two separate shocks here.
  • The first shock is that we now, finally, know the right answer to this central mystery of algorithms going back to the late 1960s, when Volker Strassen first showed that ω<3 (he got ω≤log27) … and that the true answer is actually a rational number (!).
