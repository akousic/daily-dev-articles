## Summary

Ampersand from Humans& is now available on AI Gateway for free through November 13, 2026. Ampersand is a model for group conversations. Humans& built Ampersand on Z.ai's open-weight GLM 5.3 and trained it with reinforcement learning in simulated group conversations that run for up to months of simulated time.

## Key Takeaways

- Most chat models are built for one person asking a question and the model answering it.
- Ampersand is trained to take part in a conversation between several people.
- It reads every message, including the ones not addressed to it, and after each one it decides whether to reply or stay quiet.
