## Summary

Yesterday I resigned from the Swift Android workgroup. I’ve received a number of inquiries about this, and rather than responding individually to each one, I figured I would just write up a post with my reasoning and submit it to the public record. First off, my resignation was not triggered by any sort of conflict, grievance, or specific incident.

## Key Takeaways

- Early Swift community efforts got off to a rough start when Swift language creator Chris Lattner quit due to what he described as a “toxic environment”.
- This event predates my direct involvement in the project, so I am not privy to the specifics.
- But I want to be clear that this was in no way my experience: everyone I interacted with, both from Apple and external organizations, both in meetings and in forum discussions and direct messages, have been polite, courteous, and the paragon of civility and professional conduct.
