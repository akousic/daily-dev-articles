## Summary

Cloudflare has announced the general availability of its Internal DNS service, a cloud-native authoritative and recursive DNS for private networks. The announcement has come after a period of private and public beta testing that started in June 2025. According to the company, the new capability will simplify how IT teams and companies operate and manage private DNS workloads.

## Key Takeaways

- It promises to consolidate both private and public DNSs onto a single control plane, with a unified API, audit trail, and policy registry.
- In the announcement, Enrique Somoza, product manager at Cloudflare, and Hannes Gerhart, senior product manager at Cloudflare, explain how the service is designed.
- Cloudflare Internal DNS consists of two components: Gateway Resolver and Internal Authoritative DNS.
