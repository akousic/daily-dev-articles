## Summary

Training can act as a confidence engine, turning repeated behaviors into habits and culture. In her talk, Engineer Confidence with Reliable Training Programs at GOTO Copenhagen, Jennifer Petoff showed how we can tailor training programs that equip developers and operators with the skills to run their services in line with the organization’s maturity and growth, and apply SRE principles to the training itself. Software ownership is a human challenge as much as it is a technical one.

## Key Takeaways

- Most formal education programs are geared towards the first 10% of the software development life cycle, the build phase, Petoff said.
- Engineers and operators are tasked with maintaining, debugging, securing, and evolving systems they may not even have written: There’s this implicit assumption that coding proficiency translates seamlessly to operational confidence, but it doesn’t.
- Without systematic training, this may create imposter syndrome, stress, and maybe even attrition in your organization.
