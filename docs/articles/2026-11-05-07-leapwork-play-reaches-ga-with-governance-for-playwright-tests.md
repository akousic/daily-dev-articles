## Summary

Leapwork made Play generally available on September 30, 2026 for teams managing Playwright test suites. The service adds requirement traceability and approval workflows around standard Playwright code, which teams can run in their existing continuous integration (CI) pipelines or through Leapwork's hosted execution service. In its announcement, Leapwork says Play imports existing Playwright suites without changes.

## Key Takeaways

- Engineers can connect Jira and Git repositories, along with product documentation, to build a knowledge base that supplies context for generated tests.
- They can also record a browser journey and turn the recording into a test.
- Leapwork says Play monitors connected repositories for pushes that change application behaviour or the interface, then recommends corresponding test updates.
