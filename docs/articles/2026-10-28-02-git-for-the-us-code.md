## Key Takeaways

- The US code Outside the titles Congress has enacted as positive law, the code is only prima facie evidence of the law, and the Statutes at Large govern where the two differ.[1] The Office of the Law Revision Counsel publishes it as a series of release points, each current through a named public law, and keeps reorganising it; a bill to enact title 41 as positive law was introduced in September 2025.[2] Which public law wrote a sentence is recorded in amendment notes, in prose.
- When a change took effect is often in a separate effective-date note, and where Congress said nothing the default rule is that a law takes effect on the day it is enacted.[3] The definition of a small business debtor in 11 USC § 1182(1) shows how far apart those answers can be.
