## Summary

LLM Policies: Progress At All Costs GNOME and KDE have started to consider LLM policies, and we should talk about what this is really about. KDE caught everyone's attention first by igniting a flame war with an LLM-friendly draft that ended up being deleted, causing a few bans, and having a bunch of people go full "Some of you may die, but that is a sacrifice I am willing to make". GNOME has not proposed anything official, but some teams (gnome-calendar, loupe, libadwaita, gnome-software, Circle, among others) already have strong policies in place, and there is now an informal draft to ban all LLM contributions to GNOME projects and infrastructure.
