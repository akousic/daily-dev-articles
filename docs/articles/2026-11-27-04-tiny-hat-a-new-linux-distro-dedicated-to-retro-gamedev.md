## Summary

Tiny Hat - A Linux distro for 32-bit and 64-bit x86 computers. - Intended for retro gaming and retro game development (DOS, GBA, SNES, NES, TIC-80, CHIP-8, C64 and ZX Spectrum). - The image is signed and fully reproducible.

- - Works better than MS-DOS and FreeDOS for playing games on the old x86 retro gaming computer I created the distro for (because of hardware support).
