## Summary

An open-source AI accelerator, developed by AI. openTPU brings the lessons of auto-arch-tournament to AI accelerators. It asks two questions: how far can AI agents go at hardware design, and can they build the chip that runs their own inference?

## Key Takeaways

- otpu-chat running LFM2.5-230M on the FPGA card (left), with otpu-smi showing the card's utilization and DRAM bandwidth (right).
- openTPU is also a learning project.
- The whole accelerator lives in one small monorepo that you can read end to end: the hardware design (SystemVerilog), the instruction set, a bit-exact simulator, a kernel language and its compiler, and the host software that drives a real PCIe card.
