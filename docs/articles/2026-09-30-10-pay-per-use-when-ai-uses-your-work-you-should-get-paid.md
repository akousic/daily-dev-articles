## Summary

Pay Per Use: when AI uses your work, you should get paid AI answer engines read a publisher’s page and hand the reader a summary, so the visit, and the revenue that would come with it, never happens. Most publishers will never sign a licensing deal with the companies that use their work in AI products, and no company can negotiate with millions of sites. The web needs a way to say “yes, if you pay.” Pay Per Use is one way to say it, and it's now in beta.
