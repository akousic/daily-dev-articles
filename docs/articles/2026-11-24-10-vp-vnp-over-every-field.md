## Summary

For every field, circuits for the Hamiltonian cycle polynomial, and for the permanent outside characteristic 2, need more than m^(c (log m)^(1/3)) gates for all large m. The same holds for approximating circuits. So VP is a proper subset of VNP, and VNP is not contained in the closure of VP.

## Key Takeaways

- This does not settle P vs NP.
