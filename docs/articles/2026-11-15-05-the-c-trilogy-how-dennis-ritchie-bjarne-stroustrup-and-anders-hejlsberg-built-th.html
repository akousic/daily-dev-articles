Summary

Almost every piece of software running on Earth today traces back to three people trying to solve the exact same headache: how to write large, complex programs without accidentally corrupting memory. If you booted up a laptop today, opened a browser, played a 3D game, or swiped a credit card, you're running code shaped by Dennis Ritchie, Bjarne Stroustrup, and Anders Hejlsberg. Together, they created the most famous trilogy in programming history: C, C++, and C#.
