# Restricting AI agents in mobile operating systems platforms from capturing data

## Summary

Privacy is our mission; it’s why we exist, and we do everything we can to protect it. This means that we are restricting the latest AI agents built into mobile operating systems from potentially capturing and storing your Signal data. We’ve already had to do this once with Windows.

## Key Takeaways

- Last year, we used screenshot blocking to prevent Microsoft’s Recall AI from taking and storing screenshots of your Signal messages on Windows 11 operating systems by default.1 We will now be taking similar measures to restrict agentic systems built into iOS 27 and on Android versions where we can detect an AI assistant attempting to read your Signal screen.2 The problem is similar to Microsoft Recall on Windows: Google and Apple are integrating agentic capabilities like Gemini Intelligence with Google Gemini and Onscreen Awareness with Siri AI deep into their operating systems.
- This is a significant move.
- Google (Android), Apple (iOS/macOS), and Microsoft (Windows) produce the three major consumer operating systems (OS), which comprise the environment that third-party developers – like Signal – build their apps to function and operate within.
