## Key Takeaways

- Earlier this week, Anthropic stated in their S1 that they “may imminently meet” the threshold of “dramatically accelerated progress due to AI” as defined in their Responsible Scaling Policy.
- In their latest RSP (effective July 2026), say this threshold will be met when: - They observe or expect overall capabilities progress to be going twice as fast as the fastest sustained rate before AI was contributing much to AI R&D.2 - This is plausibly attributable to AI automating AI R&D, rather than acceleration in compute or other inputs.3 For the last few months, some of my colleagues at METR have been working on a deep-access investigation to understand how much Anthropic is being accelerated by its AI systems.
- From my reading of their report and Anthropic’s RSP, it looks like the “dramatic acceleration” threshold has already been crossed.4 The report authors found that: - Between October 2025 and October 2026, capabilities progress at Anthropic has been going about twice as fast as it had been over the previous twelve months.
