As good news to the months of headaches over California's Digital Age Assurance Act and concerns around its impact on the Linux/open-source world, AB-1856 has finally passed which now amends protections and exemptions for easing compliance of open-source platforms. Per the bill's text, a key addition is: "(2) “Operating system provider” does not mean a person or entity that distributes an operating system or application under license terms that permit a recipient to copy, redistribute, and modify the software." Thereby exempting Linux distributions, BSDs, and others distributed under open-source licenses.
